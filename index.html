02 / Approach

Follow the question
through the data.

Every result should have a clear definition and a path back to its source.

  1. 01

    Ask a specific question

    Which categories bring in revenue, which earn stronger margins, and where do returns deserve a closer look?

  2. 02

    Prepare the source

    Review duplicates, reject invalid records, standardise text, and document assumptions about missing values.

  3. 03

    Check the calculations

    Reconcile SQL totals and Excel formulas. Calculate overall rates from matching totals.

  4. 04

    Explain the findings

    Use readable charts, practical follow-up questions, and clear limits on what the data can tell us.
